Currently there are 4 ways, how a particle can be redered by the client. These are `Blend`, `BlendAdd`, `Erosion` and `Distortion`

### Blend
The texture of the partice gets mixed with the background 
### BlendAdd
The texture of the particle gets additevely mixed with the background
### Erosion
The texture of the particle overwrites the background. Bright colours additionally have a glow effect around them (semitransparent; does not affect world light, purely visual)
