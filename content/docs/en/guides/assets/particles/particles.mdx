## What are particles
Particles are 2D textures that get spawned in a region, defined in the `ParticleSpawner` Asset. It also defines the size and stretch, rendering mode, orientation/rotation, texture distortion, tint, amount of particles and their lifespan, reactions to the enviroment and movement. One layer above is the `ParticleSpawnerGroup`. It defines the created amount of a specific spawner, it's lifespan, movement and rotation. They are or their own asset but rather the main part of the `ParticleSystem`, a container for multiple particles, making overlaying `ParticleSpawners` for enhanced effects easier. The particles that get attached to `Items`, `ModelAssets`, `RootInteractions`, `EntityEffects`, etc. as well as the ones spawned are of this type. You cannot spawn ParticleSpawners on their own, so I advise you to make a debug ParticleSystem, where you can add your particle to test them, but more on the workflow is [here](Workflow).
